3 Enter the plans to compare

Add your current plan and any others you're considering. For time-of-use plans, define the peak/shoulder/off-peak windows below — anything not covered by a window uses the plan's default rate.

Load a published plan

Pre-fill rates straight from the public Consumer Data Right plan data . Pick one, then tweak anything you like — it becomes an ordinary editable plan below.

Your plans are saved automatically in this browser, so they'll still be here next time. Use Export to back them up or move them to another device.

5 Can you make it cheaper? Shift your load

Time-of-use plans reward you for using power when it's cheap. This looks at how much of your usage lands in expensive peak/shoulder periods, and estimates what you'd save by moving some of that to off-peak — for example running the dishwasher, washing machine, dryer, pool pump or EV charger overnight.

Where your money goes, by rate period

Annual general-usage cost grouped by the rate that applies. Tall expensive bars are your best load-shifting targets.
